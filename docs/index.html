Features

One project, every view linked.

A measurement selected in one view is selected in all of them, with the same filters, sets and colours.

Logging and the table

Alpha, beta and gamma become true orientations as you type. The table stays quick at tens of thousands of rows.

Stereonet and statistics

Density contours, sets, Fisher and Bingham statistics and eigenvectors, compiled hole by hole.

Sections and drawings

Vertical, plan and inclined sections with corridors, traced polylines and DXF export.

Modelling

Surfaces, bodies and faults ordered by age, with offsets, ended at the topography and the model volume.

3D view and downhole logs

Holes, discs, models, meshes and rasters in one scene, and strip logs side by side.

Import, export and reports

CSV, Excel, DXF, shapefiles and GeoTIFF in. Tables, figures, linework and model reports out.

Free

The stereonet is free, for good.

A stereonet project takes field measurements or any orientation data, with contouring, sets, statistics and figure export. It needs no licence and never expires, so it serves for teaching, mapping and a quick look at any data set.
